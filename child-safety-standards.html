Meetania Child Safety Standards

Effective date: 27 November 2026

Meetania is committed to protecting children from child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE). We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, sexual exploitation, or any behaviour that endangers children.

Adults only: Meetania is intended for users aged 18 and above. Child sexual abuse and exploitation are strictly prohibited on our platform.

1. Prohibited content and behaviour

Users must not:

  • Create, upload, request, share or distribute CSAM.
  • Sexually exploit or abuse anyone under 18.
  • Engage in grooming or sexual solicitation of minors.
  • Use Meetania to facilitate child trafficking or exploitation.
  • Attempt to contact or target minors for sexual purposes.

2. Reporting child safety concerns

Users can report inappropriate profiles, content and behaviour through Meetania's in-app reporting feature. Child safety concerns can also be sent to our designated contact:

Email: poonam.agrawal5001@gmail.com

Please include relevant details and links or profile information where available. Do not download or forward suspected CSAM.

3. Review and enforcement

Meetania will review child safety reports and take appropriate action. This may include removing prohibited content, restricting access, suspending or terminating accounts, and preserving relevant information where legally appropriate.

4. Reporting to authorities

Meetania will report confirmed CSAM and suspected child exploitation to the appropriate regional or national authorities as required by applicable law. We will cooperate with lawful investigations.

5. Age restrictions

Meetania is intended exclusively for adults aged 18 and above. Accounts found to violate our age requirements or child safety standards may be restricted or terminated.
