1. Introduction

Welcome to Meetania ("we", "our", or "us"). Meetania is a social networking and communication platform that helps users discover people, connect, and communicate. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how it is stored, and the choices available to you when using Meetania.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Meetania, we may collect:

  • Account information: Name, authentication details and Firebase user identifier.
  • Profile information: Profile photograph, date of birth, age, gender, biography and discovery preferences.
  • Communication information: Messages, conversation identifiers, timestamps and message delivery/read status.
  • Payment information: Purchase identifiers, product details, transaction status and paid-access validity.
  • Technical information: Information needed for security, troubleshooting and application operation.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Create and manage accounts.
  • Display profiles and help users discover people.
  • Enable messaging and communication.
  • Manage paid features and purchases.
  • Provide customer support.
  • Prevent fraud, spam, harassment and misuse.
  • Improve application security and performance.
  • Meet legal obligations.

4. Profile Visibility

Certain profile information, including your name, age, photograph, gender, biography and discovery preferences, may be visible to other users according to Meetania's features and settings. Please avoid including sensitive personal information in your public profile or messages.

5. Messages and Communication

Messages and related information may be processed and stored to provide conversation history, operate the messaging service, and support security and abuse prevention. Users should communicate responsibly and avoid sharing passwords, financial details or other sensitive information with strangers.

6. Payments and Paid Features

Meetania may offer paid access to certain communication features. Purchases made through Android or iOS applications may be handled by Google Play or Apple's applicable billing system. Payment processing is subject to the respective provider's terms and privacy policies. We do not intend to collect or store complete payment card details.

7. Third-Party Services

  • Google Firebase for authentication, database and storage.
  • Google Sign-In for authentication.
  • Apple Sign-In where supported.
  • Google Play and Apple App Store for applicable in-app purchases.

These providers process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable agreements.

8. Data Sharing

We do not sell users' personal information. Information may be shared:

  • With other users as required for social and communication features.
  • With service providers that operate the application.
  • When required by applicable law or a valid legal request.
  • When reasonably necessary to protect users, Meetania or the public.

9. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction. However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

10. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to provide our services, maintain account records, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes and enforce our policies. When an account is deleted, we will take steps to delete or anonymize associated eligible personal information, subject to applicable legal and legitimate record-retention requirements.

11. Account Deletion

Users can request deletion through the Delete Account option in the application. We will take steps to delete the account and associated eligible personal information, subject to applicable legal, security and record-retention requirements. Certain financial or transaction records may need to be retained as required by law.

12. Children's Privacy

Meetania is intended for individuals aged 18 years and above. We do not knowingly permit individuals under 18 to use the service. If we become aware that an account belongs to an individual under 18, we may take appropriate steps to restrict or terminate that account.

13. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to:

  • Request access to your personal information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your account and eligible personal information.
  • Raise concerns about information processing.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our application, services or legal requirements. The updated version will be published with a revised "Last Updated" date.
